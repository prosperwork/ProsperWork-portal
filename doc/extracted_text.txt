Main Web App Development Blueprint Core App Dimensions (The Foundation) Dimension 1: Internal Management (The Core System):  The backend operations hub for the ProsperWork Music Concepts team, handling staff, enrolled families, leads, internal payroll, HR recruitment, and teacher operations. Dimension 2: SaaS Scheduling & Accountability Engine (Monetizable Product):  A paid, public-facing scheduling application where external parents and teachers track lessons, verify class completion for fairness, and receive automated payment and schedule reminders. Dimension 3: The Learning Hub (EdTech Ecosystem):  A three-tiered educational suite featuring: Self-Guided Learning:  Interactive games, guides, and skill-building tools (including future virtual music tools, chord references, and custom live-call visual classroom tools). AI-Assisted Learning:  AI-driven instruction modules. Premium Human Tutor:  Direct pairing with private tutors from the academy. Dimension 1: Internal Management — Functions & User Logins 1. Core Scheduling Engine, Timezone Rules & Global Profile UI Central Scheduling System:  Connects all internal academy roles with real-time updates across  Today's View ,  Weekly View  (Monday–Sunday), and the  General Scheduling Hub / Student Database . Role-Based Timezone Display: Admins & Teachers:  Always see dual timezones—their own local timezone (default Nigeria WAT) alongside the student's specific timezone. Students & Parents:  Only see their own local timezone on their dashboards (they do not see the academy's local Nigerian timezone). Global Human Profile Pictures & Student Onboarding Data: Every user across the entire platform ( Parents, Students, Teachers, Regular Admins, Head Admins, HR, QC Supervisors, Marketers/Sales, Finance, and Super Admin ) must be able to upload and display a  Profile Picture  visible across dashboards and in-app chats. Mandatory Student Onboarding Fields (DOB, Age & Social Media Consent):  Whenever an Admin or Parent creates a student profile, the form must capture: The child's  Date of Birth (DOB)  and  Age  (for automated birthday tracking and age-appropriate curriculum). A  Social Media Permission Badge ( Approved ✅  /  Private 🔒 )  indicating whether the parent consents to the child's practice/class videos being posted on ProsperWork's social media platforms. Core Function — Student Continuity & Scheduling Engine)** * **Permanent Child & Lesson Attendance History Log:** * Every lesson action across the system (*Completed/Done, Missed, Canceled, or Rescheduled*) is automatically and permanently saved into a timestamped **Attendance & Lesson History Log**. * **Dedicated Per-Child History Log:** Every child profile has its own permanent, month-by-month historical ledger logging every class the child has ever taken or missed, alongside the exact dates and timestamps. * **Admin Approval Audit Trail ("Did This Class Really Happen?"):** Whenever a Teacher marks a class "Done" and an Admin approves it (or when an Admin directly marks a class "Done"), the system permanently logs the exact date, time, teacher, and approving Admin in a verification record so staff can always audit past classes and calculate monthly counts and payroll with 100% accuracy. 2. Multi-Role Authentication & Departmental Portals The system must support dedicated logins and role-specific views for the following user groups: Super Admin Login:  Full, unrestricted access across all departments, financial engines, marketing funnels, HR pipelines, educational boards, system settings, and staff hierarchies (including assigning Regular Admins to Head Admins, approving teacher base-pay increases, and approving final monthly payroll). (Future Scalability Tier — General Manager Login):  Reserved architectural tier above Head Admins to oversee multiple Head Admins as the academy scales globally. Head Admin / Admin Manager Login (Admins Managing Admins):  Dedicated portal for senior admins who oversee assigned groups of Regular Admins, co-interview Admin applicants with HR, coordinate staff leave handovers with HR, step in to start/usher classes if a Regular Admin is unreachable, assign parents to Regular Admins, coordinate trial/permanent handovers and win-back calls with Sales, review monthly admin reports, and resolve parent disputes. Regular Admin Login:  Portal for managing their strictly assigned portfolio of parents/families, schedules, full teacher directory access, classroom ushering, and day-to-day student/teacher operations. Human Resources (HR) Department Login:  Dedicated portal for recruiting and onboarding all staff (Teachers, Regular Admins, Marketers, Social Media, etc.), coordinating interviews with QC and Head Admins, negotiating initial compensation, and approving staff leave/breaks. Teacher Supervisor / Quality Control (QC) Login:  Dedicated portal for the Quality Control department to co-interview and train new teachers with HR, upload finalized teacher onboarding data, monitor active teachers, track teacher retention/performance metrics, observe class activities, manage curriculum, and verify curriculum compliance. Teacher Login:  Portal for academy teachers to log in and access their account status, student statuses, schedules, curriculum database, earnings breakdown (Standard, Trial, and Cover classes), and student boards. Parent Login:  Portal for enrolled academy parents to log in, accept digital terms, refer friends, and view their children's schedules, progress, multi-currency billing, and senior manager escalation controls. Student Login:  Dedicated portal for students (linked directly to their parent's profile) where children can log in independently to view homework, their live lesson board, digital notebook, song bank, practice videos, and personal learning details. Finance / Accounting Department Login:  Dedicated portal to view and manage incoming multi-currency due amounts from parents, receive newly onboarded staff/teacher base rates from HR/QC, propose teacher pay adjustments (subject to Super Admin approval), and prepare outgoing monthly payroll for teachers and admins. Marketing & Sales Department Login (Unified Lead, Trial, Retention & Social Media Hub):  Shared portal for Marketers, Sales reps, and Social Media managers to track leads with response timers, nurture cold leads, coordinate trial classes with the Head Admin, close first-time payments, conduct parent win-back/exit feedback calls, and access approved student/teacher videos for social media promotion. 3. Centralized Curriculum & Resource Database (Teacher, QC & Super Admin Hub) Global Teaching Repository:  A centralized database accessible to teachers once they log into a class. The  Super Admin  and  Quality Control (QC)  team can create, upload, update, add, and remove songs, structured curriculum guides, and instructional materials here so teachers can pull resources directly during lessons to teach students. 4. Student Continuity Data Center, Digital Notebook & Live Lesson Board (OneNote-Style) "Medical Chart" Student Continuity:  Every student must have a dedicated, permanent learning dossier. Just like a hospital patient chart, if a student is permanently reassigned to a new teacher (or taken by a Cover Teacher for a single day), that teacher can immediately see everything the child has already covered and where to continue without starting over. Live Syncing Workspace (Auto-Saving History):  To ensure lesson history is captured automatically without relying on teachers remembering to fill out post-class reports, the system must feature a OneNote-style interactive writer/board used live during the lesson. As the teacher types lesson notes, topics, and exercises (e.g.,  "Today we are learning Mary Had a Little Lamb..." ) or shares their screen, the student sees it updating in real time on their own app/screen like a live classroom board. Everything written on this board automatically saves to the child's permanent history, logging both  Completed Curriculum Topics  and  Upcoming/To-Do Topics  for future lessons. Student Digital Notebook & Personal Library:  The Data Center also serves as the student's permanent digital notebook and library. Even if the child does not have a physical notebook, they can log into their account at any time to access all original teacher notes, taught songs, class activities, and sent videos in one place. Practice Videos & Structured Homework Exchange: Teacher-to-Student:  Teachers can send homework assignments as well as practice and tutorial videos directly to the student's Data Center. Student-to-Teacher ("Send Homework" Feature):  Students can submit completed homework back to the teacher through a dedicated  "Send Homework"  button/portal in their dashboard (structured submission rather than direct open chat). Student Song Bank & Performance Scoring Personal Song Bank:  Each student profile must include a Song Bank where the child or teacher can list songs the student wants to learn, organized into two clear categories: Songs Done  (Completed repertoire) Songs To Do  (Upcoming/requested songs) Student Scoring & Grading:  A built-in evaluation tool allowing teachers to score and grade the student's progress and performance on lessons and songs. 6. Departmental Screen Specifications A. Marketing, Sales & Social Media Department Screen (Lead-to-Enrollment & Retention Funnel): Unified Marketing & Sales Board (Scalable for Sub-Departments):  Connects Marketers (ad strategy/lead capture), Sales Reps (calling leads, booking trials, closing enrollments), and the Social Media team in one shared workspace integrated with Facebook Lead funnels / Google Sheets, architected so Sales and Marketing can be split into separate sub-departments as the team grows. "Speed-to-Lead" Live Timer & Rep Response Tracker: Every incoming lead displays a live  "Time Since Lead Arrived"  timer and logs the exact timestamp of the Sales Rep's first response, allowing management to see which reps reply immediately vs. which reps reply late. Lead Status, Parent Referrals & Callback Reminders: Sales/Marketers update lead statuses ( Booked ,  Not Booked ,  Call Now , etc.), log notes, set timed callback alerts, and message leads directly via in-app  WhatsApp and Email . Automatically receives  Parent Referral Leads  submitted from the Parent Dashboard, badged as  "Referral from [Parent Name]" . Cold Lead Nursery & Community / Testimonial Broadcast Bucket: Unbooked leads (e.g., parents who say  "Call me next month"  or don't convert immediately after a trial) are stored in a  "Nursery / Follow-Up Bucket." Marketers can group these leads into a community/broadcast list and send 1-click weekly WhatsApp/Email blasts featuring student performance videos, testimonials, and special offers to warm them up until they enroll. End-to-End Trial Class & Handover Workflow: Trial Booking Request to Head Admin:  When a lead is ready for a Trial Class, the Sales rep selects the parent's preferred trial dates/times and submits a  Trial Booking Request  to the  Head Admin (Admin Manager) . Head Admin Assignment:  The Head Admin assigns a  Regular Admin  (to usher/anchor the trial) and a  Teacher  (to teach the trial). Post-Trial Follow-Up Alert:  Once the Regular Admin marks the Trial Class as  "Done & Verified" , the Sales/Marketing board receives an immediate notification. 24-Hour Sales Conversion Check-In:  The Sales rep reaches out to the parent to ask how the trial went, sends the  PDF Parent Agreement & Terms of Service  via WhatsApp/Email, and secures their agreement to continue with a permanent schedule. Permanent Handover to Head Admin:  Once the parent agrees to continue, the Sales rep hands the parent over to the  Head Admin , who assigns a permanent  Managing Admin (Regular Admin)  and permanent  Teacher  (or allows the Regular Admin to select the teacher). First-Payment Collection & Finance Handover:  As soon as the permanent lesson schedule is set and the  Automatic Lesson Start Stamp  triggers, the  Sales Team  is alerted to collect the parent's initial tuition payment. Once the first payment is completed, all future recurring monthly billing and follow-ups automatically transfer to the  Finance Department . Parent Win-Back & Exit Feedback Queue (Routed from Head Admin): If an enrolled parent wants to quit/pause or is unhappy, the  Head Admin  can route that parent back to the  Sales Team  to gather exit feedback and reconvert the family. Social Media Content Feed (With Consent Filter): The Social Media Manager receives routed  student homework/practice videos ,  teacher videos , and  class clips  clearly badged with the child's  Social Media Permission Status ( Approved ✅  /  Private 🔒 )  so only approved videos are posted publicly. Future CRM Analytics (Phase 2 Note):  Capability to expand into full CRM analytics later to evaluate lead performance and ad ROI. B. Finance / Accounting Department Screen & Payroll Engine: Automatic "Lesson Start" Billing Trigger: Whenever a converted student's permanent schedule is created (post-trial), the system logs an  Automatic Lesson Start Timestamp , alerting both  Sales  (for initial payment collection) and  Finance  (setting the recurring monthly billing anchor date). Configurable Multi-Currency Parent Billing (£ GBP, $ USD, € EUR, $ CAD, ₦ NGN): Based on the parent's billing agreement, parents can choose to pay in  International Currency (e.g., $, £, €)  or  Local Currency (₦ Naira) . Finance staff can toggle a parent's billing currency at any time. Multi-Currency Dashboard Totals & Naira Equivalent:  The Finance (and Super Admin) dashboard displays separate real-time subtotals for each currency ( Total USD  $ ,  Total GBP  £ ,  Total EUR  € ,  Total NGN  ₦ ) alongside the  automatic Naira ( ₦ ) equivalent  for every transaction and a combined grand total. Parent Payment Tracking, Referral Discounts & Due-Date Views: Finance staff see all parent payments categorized by due status:  Due Today ,  Due Tomorrow ,  Due Next Tomorrow , and  Overdue  (with exact days overdue). Automatically alerts Finance to apply a  Referral Discount  when a parent's referred friend completes their first month's payment. Automated & Manual WhatsApp Reminders:  Includes 1-click WhatsApp integration so Finance staff can manually message overdue or due parents alongside automated system reminders. Configurable Billing Terms (Upfront vs. After-Service):  Finance staff can toggle each parent's billing terms between  Upfront Payment  (paid before lessons begin/continue) and  After-Service Payment  (paid at the end of the monthly lesson cycle). Payment Verification & Gateway Readiness: Current Manual Receipt Flow:  Parents mark "Paid" on their portal and upload their transfer receipt. Finance staff verify the receipt and tick "Paid" to reset the parent's billing cycle to zero. Card Gateway Integration (Phase 2 Target):  Built to support automated international bank card payment processors. Cash Flow, Expenses & Tax Calculator (With Owner Profit Privacy Lock): Tracks all  Money In  and  Money Out  alongside a configurable  Tax Engine . 2-Tier Profit Visibility Lock:  Regular Finance staff view operational billing, currency totals, expenses, and payroll calculations, while the  Executive Net Profit / Total Gain Summary  is restricted to the  Super Admin  by default (with a Super Admin toggle to grant/revoke visibility). HR/QC-to-Finance Base-Pay Setup & Super Admin Raise Approval: Initial Onboarding Pay Setup:  When a new teacher or staff member completes onboarding with HR and QC, their agreed initial  Base Rate and Bank Details  route directly to the Finance dashboard. Pay Increase / Promotion Approval Gate:  Finance staff can input a base-pay increase for an existing teacher (e.g., for a promotion); however, any pay increase stays in  "Pending Approval"  status until  approved by the Super Admin . Admin & Management Payroll (With Extra-Parent Capacity Bonus): Calculates monthly salaries for Regular Admins, Head Admins, and staff. Whenever a Regular Admin is assigned active parents  above their agreed base quota  (e.g., above 12 or 24 parents, including temporary leave coverage), the system automatically calculates their  per-parent extra payout . Teacher Payroll Calculation Engine (3-Tier Earnings & Maker-Checker Approval): Dual-Confirmation Attendance Rule:  A lesson counts toward payroll when  Teacher marks "Done" + Admin approves , OR when an  Admin directly marks "Done" . 1. Standard Lesson Pay & Weekly Frequency Multiplier:  Fixed monthly base rate per assigned student for 1x/week (e.g., ₦20,000 or ₦25,000), scaling by weekly frequency (1x, 2x, 3x/week). Start-Date Pro-Rating (4-Part Division with Exact Date Visibility):  Mid-month starts display the exact start date and week tier ( Week 1:  4/4 |  Week 2:  3/4 |  Week 3:  2/4 |  Week 4:  1/4). 2. Trial Class Pay & 3. Cover Class Pay:  Tracked separately and added to the teacher's month-end payout. Completion Threshold & 28th-to-28th Rollover Rule:  Completing 4+ lessons (for 1x/week) earns full pay; fewer than 4 pays strictly for parts completed. Payroll executes on the  28th : classes falling on the 28th–31st for faithful teachers are paid in advance, and if missed on the 28th–31st, are automatically deducted in the next 28th–28th cycle. Manual Bonus & Deduction Adjustments (With Reason Logging):  Finance can add bonuses or deduct loans/penalties with a mandatory  Reason  field. Maker-Checker Final Payroll Approval:  Finance submits the 28th monthly payroll batch to the  Super Admin  for  Final Approval  before disbursement (with a vacation auto-approve toggle). C. Student Login & Student Screen: Parent-Linked Sub-Account, Profile Picture & Unique Code Authentication: Standalone Student Login:  Auto-generates a unique Student Username (e.g.,  PW-Best482 ) delivered to the Parent's Dashboard. The parent/admin sets the password, and the child can upload their profile picture. 1-Click "Switch to Kid Mode" (PIN-Protected):  Parents can switch their app into Kid Mode with 1 click, requiring the  4-digit Parent PIN  to return to the Parent Screen. Daily/Weekly Schedule & Dynamic "Join Class Now" Button (Time-Locked Window): Displays  Today's View  and  Weekly View  (in student's local timezone only). The  "Join Class Now"  button unlocks  2 hours before  class and remains open for a  5-to-6-hour window  before locking again. Practice Timer, Streak Gamification & In-App Studio Recorder: Students log home practice minutes, build streaks, and record video/audio directly in-app to submit to the  Central Homework & Media Board  (routed to Teacher, Admin, and Social Media). Homework Center, Learning Materials & 2go-Style Rank Progression: Displays homework with automated reminders, Live Lesson Notebook, Song Bank, class recordings, and tiered  2go-Style Achievement Ranks/Badges  ( Novice → Beginner → Amateur → Master ) unlocked by the Teacher, QC Supervisor, or Super Admin. D. Parent Login & Parent Screen: Multi-Method Authentication, Pre-Lesson Digital Terms & Security PIN: Parents log in via  Username, Email, or Phone Number  + Password, upload their profile picture, and set a  4-digit Security PIN . Pre-Lesson Digital Terms & Policy Gate:  Parents receive the  Parent Agreement, Terms of Service & Reschedule Policy  as a  PDF  via WhatsApp/Email to read thoroughly, and must click  "I Agree / Accept"  inside the app upon first login. Multi-Child Overview, Social Media Consent & Rank Visibility: Displays all enrolled children (with DOB/Age and Social Media Permission toggle), current  Rank/Badge Level , and mastered songs list. Multi-Currency Billing, Due-Date Tracker & Downloadable PDF Invoices: Displays due dates and bills in the parent's chosen currency ( £, $, €, or ₦ ), allows receipt uploads (or card payment in Phase 2), and provides downloadable  PDF Receipts/Invoices . Built-In "Refer a Friend & Get a Discount" Button: Parents can share a unique referral link or input a friend's contact details directly in-app. The referred friend routes straight into the  Marketing Lead Funnel , and the parent receives an automatic tuition discount once the friend pays. Today's & Weekly Schedule View + Lesson Counter (Transparency Tracker): Displays  Today's View ,  Weekly View , and a live monthly counter:  Classes Taken This Month  vs.  Classes Remaining  ( Completed ,  Rescheduled ,  Canceled ,  Paused ). Multi-Slot Reschedule Request with 1–2 Hour Notice Lock & Admin Approval: Locks  1 to 2 hours before  class. Allows selecting single or multiple available dates/times for Admin and Teacher approval. Direct Admin Messaging & Senior Manager / Unresponsive Admin Escalation: Includes  "Message Admin"  and a  "Request Senior Manager Call / Class Not Started Alert"  button routing directly to the  Head Admin . Mirrored Child Learning Inbox, Birthday Celebrations & Monthly PDF Report Card: Mirrors all child homework/notes/videos, delivers birthday cards + video compilations, and generates downloadable  Monthly Progress Report Cards (PDF) .
